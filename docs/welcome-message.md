# Welcome to TrailTalk 👋

A place for Salesforce people to ask questions, share what they’ve built, and help each other get unstuck.

Introduce yourself, find your communities, and bring your questions—even the ones you think you should already know the answer to. Built something useful? Show it off in **Flow ’n Tell**.

Be generous with what you know, respectful when you disagree, and careful with customer data. We’re here to learn from each other.

**Your people. Your platform. Welcome to TrailTalk.**
