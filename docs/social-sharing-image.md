Typography must be exceptionally clean, highly legible when downscaled. Keep all text and essential imagery at least 80 pixels inside the edges of a 1200x630 composition. No screenshot frame, browser chrome, buttons, watermark, photographic stock look, or UI mockup. Premium finished branded artwork.

## Updating the artwork

Save future artwork under a new versioned filename and update `socialImage` in `app/layout.tsx`. Social apps cache previews independently; existing shared messages may retain their previous preview until that service refreshes it.
